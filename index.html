Important Network Announcement

It has now been nearly 20 days since the Infinium network last produced a block. At this moment, there are no active miners, and the blockchain has effectively come to a complete standstill.

During this time, I have waited in the hope that mining activity would return. Unfortunately, that has not happened.

A blockchain cannot operate without miners securing the network and validating new blocks. Without active miners, transactions cannot be confirmed, the chain cannot progress, and the network is no longer functional.

Continuing to maintain the infrastructure, servers, and development of an inactive network is no longer financially or technically sustainable.

Unless mining activity resumes immediately, I will have no choice but to begin shutting down Infinium's operations.

A Final Opportunity

I sincerely appreciate everyone who has supported Infinium throughout its journey. If the community decides to bring miners back online and restore activity to the network, I am willing to reconsider this decision.

The future of Infinium depends entirely on the participation of its community. Without active miners, it is simply not possible to keep the project alive.

Thank you for your support.
The Infinium Team
